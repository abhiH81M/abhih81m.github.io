How It Works

From Creation to Results in Minutes

A simple classroom workflow for teachers — create, share, conduct, collect, and review results locally.

📝

Create Your Exam

Create MCQ questions, True/False questions, quick polls, and class tests. Set the exam duration and prepare the paper on the teacher device.

2
📡

Integrity

Auto-Submit on Exit

A practical classroom control that helps teachers receive student progress if a student leaves the exam screen.

Current Answers Can Be Submitted

If a student leaves the Exam Maker: Offline Tests exam screen during a test, the answers completed so far may be submitted so the teacher can still receive that student’s progress.

  • 🔔 Teachers can ask students to enable Do Not Disturb before the exam to reduce accidental interruptions.
  • ⚡ The app is designed to submit current answers when a student leaves the exam screen.
  • 📤 The teacher can receive the submitted progress through the available local collection method.
  • 📱 Teachers can review submitted progress and final results in the results view.
