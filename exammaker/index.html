FAQ

Common Questions

Everything teachers ask before using Exam Maker: Offline Tests in their classroom.

Can I conduct a classroom exam without internet?

Yes — the core exam flow in Exam Maker: Offline Tests works without internet. Create the test, share by QR or Nearby, students answer on their phones, and results are collected locally on the teacher device.

Do students need to create accounts to take the exam?

No. Students only enter their name or roll number to join. There are no sign-ups, emails, or passwords required — making it fast to get a full class started.

How do students receive the exam on their phones?

Students join by scanning a QR code shown on the teacher's device, or through Nearby wireless sharing when the device supports it. Both methods work without internet.

What question types are supported?

Exam Maker supports MCQ (multiple choice) and True/False questions. You can also run quick polls and track class attendance from the same app.

What happens if a student's phone gets a notification mid-exam?

If a student leaves the exam screen, their current answers are automatically submitted. The teacher still receives their progress. It's recommended to ask students to enable Do Not Disturb before the exam.

Is Exam Maker: Offline Tests free?

Yes, the app is free to download on Android. Some export or advanced features may be available through in-app purchase.

Can I use it as a quiz maker or student response system?

Yes. Teachers can create quizzes, quick polls, MCQ tests, and True/False exams, then collect student responses locally.
