Data, ads, and the practical bits.

This page is meant to stay practical. Lotus stores its core tracker, task, timer, and settings data on-device. Internet access is mainly used for ads, purchases, and opening hosted web pages when needed.

Data

Local-first by default

Habits, daily statuses, timer settings, tasks, and most app settings stay on the device. You can start using Lotus immediately without creating an account.

Ads

Rewarded ads for analytics access

In the free version, Google AdMob rewarded ads may be used before opening analytics screens. AdMob can collect limited device, network, and ad interaction data.

Purchases

Google Play handles billing

Remove-ads purchases are processed by Google Play. Lotus does not handle or store your payment card information directly.

Quick answers.

These are the points most people look for before installing: what the app does, how it stores data, and whether it adds friction.

FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. Lotus is designed so you can add habits, log your day, manage tasks, and use the timer right away.

FAQ

Does the app work offline?

Core tracking, daily logging, pomodoro usage, and to-do management work offline. Internet is mainly needed for ads, purchases, and hosted web pages.

FAQ

Can I move my data later?

Yes. Lotus includes encrypted backup and restore so you can keep a copy of your data or move it to another device later.
